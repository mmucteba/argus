ARGUS T18

Low-cost automated pathology slide scanner, METU EE493 capstone. A two-axis stage moves the slide under a fixed zoom lens and a Raspberry Pi HQ camera; an Arduino with GRBL drives the motors and a laptop stitches the tiles and runs the tumour classifier. This page rebuilds itself after every change and every morning.
