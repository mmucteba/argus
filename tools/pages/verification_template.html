How a part becomes trustworthy

  1. 1. A fact is a guessNo product picked, or only a shop page. Every part that uses it is provisional and its design task waits.
  2. 2. Verify taskPick and link the exact product, then measure it or find the maker's datasheet. Proof into evidence/, numbers into spec/facts.yaml.
  3. 3. Re-check task opensWhen every fact a part uses is verified, the audit opens a task to redo that part against the real numbers.
  4. 4. ConfirmedThe re-check stores a fingerprint of the exact values it was built on. Safe to print. If a fact it uses changes later, it goes back to step 3 by itself.

Where it lives

spec/bom.yaml is what we buy and where. spec/facts.yaml holds every real-world size with its status, source and the item it comes from (buy:). Proof goes in evidence/<item>/.

What runs by itself

The audit runs every day at 07:23 and after every merge. It opens verify tasks (with the shop link and whether the product is picked), opens re-check tasks, marks design tasks waiting and updates the Status board.

What you do

Not picked: choose the exact product and put its page link in bom.yaml. Picked: find the datasheet or measure it when it arrives, put the proof in evidence/, update the fact, open a pull request. Turkish steps in REHBER.md.
