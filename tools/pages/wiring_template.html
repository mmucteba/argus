ARGUS T18 wiring

Every wire from the 12 V adapter to the camera for model v0.3 (Raspberry Pi 3B+, zoom lens, GRBL on the Uno). __NWIRES__ connections. Updated 8 Nov 2026.

First power-up, in this order

  1. Buck alone: 12 V on, nothing on its output, turn the LM2596 screw until OUT reads 5.0 V. Power off.
  2. 28BYJ-48 bipolar mod: lift the blue cap, cut the trace joining the two centre taps. Orange to pink and yellow to blue should each read one coil; leave red unconnected.
  3. Drivers into the shield with the driver's EN pin on the EN mark. Set the microstep jumpers. Plug the motors in. Never plug or unplug a motor with 12 V on.
  4. 12 V on and the Pi's USB to the Uno. Set Vref, measured from the potentiometer to GND: DRV8825 0.50 V, A4988 by its sense resistor (below).
  5. Flash GRBL 1.1 with the one-line homing edit, then paste the settings.
  6. Send ?. Push paper into each fork: Pn:X and Pn:Y must show only while blocked. If it is the other way round, set $5=1.
  7. Jog 1 mm per axis with $J=G91 X1 F300. Wrong way: flip that axis bit in $3.
  8. Home with $H. Then M8 turns the bottom LED on and M9 off.
  9. Pi: ring test at 20 % brightness, then rpicam-hello --list-cameras should list imx477.

Driver setup

X and Y · DRV8825

M0M1M21/16 step
Vref 0.50 VI = 2 x Vref = 1.0 A

17HS4401 is rated 1.5 A; 1.0 A keeps the motors cool with a 15x torque margin. Stick the heatsink on. The DRV8825 needs 12 V on to read Vref.

Z · A4988

0.1 A targetVref = 8 x Rs x I

Vref 0.054 V
R050

Read the tiny resistor next to the chip. The 28BYJ coil limits the current on its own too, so near the bottom of the pot is fine. 4076 half-steps per turn and the 0.7 mm M4 thread give __ZSTEPS__ steps/mm, 0.17 um each.

The A socket stays empty, no clone jumpers.

Pi setup

# /boot/firmware/config.txt
dtparam=spi=on
core_freq=250      # pi 3: keeps the spi clock right for the ring

Add the user to the gpio group so the ring runs without root. Python: picamera2 (preinstalled), pyserial for GRBL at 115200 baud, rpi_ws281x in SPI mode on GPIO10.

GRBL 1.1 settings

Before uploading grblUpload from the Arduino IDE, edit config.h so homing skips Z (there is no Z switch). Then paste the settings into the serial console at 115200 baud.
